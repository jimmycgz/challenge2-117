06 · Rigor & Judgment · Data Validation Slide 6 / 8

Four Data Traps Caught by Inspecting the Tables

Operational rules stay in embeddings; census demographics are never used to rank who deserves food.

🍼 1 · Infant Formula Shelf Life
❌ 1 Hour (Opened Bottle) → ✅ 8 Months (Sealed Case)

_shelf_caution flags any USDA value ≤2 hours so sealed cans aren’t rejected.

🧊 2 · Kitchen Fridge vs. Walk-In
❌ 4 Pallets → Fridge → ✅ Cap Fridge ≤ 1 Pallet

Reserves multi-pallet cold loads for commercial coolers and frozen food for freezers.

🕒 3 · Contradictory Bio Text (12 Orgs)
❌ Bio Says “2 Hours” → ✅ Enforce 19h Column

Strips timing claims from written bio quotes so they never contradict numeric hours.

📊 4 · Planted Decoys in City Counts
❌ 13 / 192 Walk-Ins → ✅ 6 / 183 Real (3.3%)

Excludes the 9 planted test cases (profile_source != 'planted') on city SQL stats.

Honest Limits Today & How We Close the Loop

A trustworthy dispatch tool states its boundaries plainly—and builds the intake loop next.

⚠️ 4 Honest Boundaries Today
1. Generated Ops Profiles: 183 IRS orgs are real, but cooler, pallets & hours are synthetic → confirm by phone.
2. Consumer USDA Guidance: Home shelf-life guidance, not a commercial cold-chain model.
3. ZIP-Centroid Coordinates: No donor address or real drive-time routing in BigQuery.
4. Stateless Single-Load Matching: One fast pantry won 3x; 5-ton farm loads aren’t split yet.
🚀 What We Build with Another 4 Hours
1. Conversational Pantry Intake Agent:
Asks “Can you take a pallet, or by hand?”, writes profile_text, and runs AI.EMBED into BigQuery immediately.
2. Multi-Pantry Load Splitting:
Split 5-ton farm loads across three 2-pallet neighborhood pantries (solving the 9% volume skew).
3. Firestore Claim Lock & Fair Rotation:
Prevent double-claiming and rotate fast pantries across the afternoon.
