1 ADK Agent + 4 Deterministic Tool Surfaces (Tested Across 42 Runs)

Passing 80 candidate rows between multiple LLM sub-agents took 76–86s per hop. Keeping candidate retrieval, hard filtering, and ranking inside deterministic Python tools cut latency to ~15.6–17.2s.

For the Spinach Offer, the Top 3 Semantic Matches Were All Wrong

Vector search finds who talks about fresh greens. Our constraint filter and tiered ranking find who can actually refrigerate and collect them within 6 hours.

❌ Ranks #1–#3 Eliminated

  • All 3 closest semantic hits needed 12h–21h to collect against a 6h clock.
  • Rank #3 (Greenline Community Pantry — planted teaching case): Begs for "fresh greens" in its profile, but has cold_storage = 'none'.

📊 Measured top_k Pool Study

  • top_k = 15 → leaves only 1 viable match
  • top_k = 40 → leaves 3 viable matches
  • top_k = 80 → leaves 12 viable matches (giving 9 backups when pantries say no).

✅ Why Ranks #50, #54, #63 Win

  • The nearest viable hit is 11th by meaning, but only has a household fridge and 1h of slack.
  • Our ranking prefers a comfortable margin (≥2h), sturdy cold storage, and own cold transport—picking #50, #54, #63.

🧪 Mutation-Tested Decoy Guarantee (selfcheck.py)

build_match_brief always includes a cold-storage failure in rejected_but_close when one exists in the pool. We wrote a mutation test in selfcheck.py that deliberately switches off the cold-storage rule to verify the test fails—which caught a real sorting bug in our first build.

6 · Engineering Rigor & Caught Data Traps Slide 6 / 8

Five Non-Obvious Traps Our Engineering Caught

Every team runs VECTOR_SEARCH. Here are five subtle failure modes in the challenge data that our code explicitly defends against:

1. The 1-Hour Infant Formula Table Bug (_shelf_caution)

USDA FoodKeeper lists Infant formula at 1 hour (the rule for a prepared bottle warmed for a baby). Our lookup_shelf_life tool warns on any figure <= 2h so sealed cases use the donor's 8-month expiry.

2. Domestic Fridge ≠ Walk-In Cooler (DOMESTIC_FRIDGE_PALLETS = 1)

A naive cold_storage != 'none' check sends 4 pallets of produce to a church with a household fridge. Our filter rejects cold_storage == 'domestic' whenever pallets > 1.

3. Sanitizing Contradictory Profile Timing Prose

Generated profile_text sometimes claims "we can be there within a couple of hours" while the numeric pickup_window_hours column is 19h. Our _excerpt() function strips timing sentences so only the numeric column governs the clock.

4. Read-Only Managed MCP + Lab Identity Guard

Ad-hoc SQL questions ("Excluding planted cases, how many recipients have a walk-in cooler?" → 6 of 183, 3.3%) use Google's Managed BigQuery MCP in read-only mode, while identity_guard.py blocks non-lab credentials.

🔍 Cold-Review Finding #1: Repeat-Winner Illusion

Per posting, means look higher-need (22% / 30%) only because one fast pantry (Shakespear Rose, in a 45% poverty / 52% no-vehicle tract) won 3 short-clock postings. Counted once per recipient (n=8), means sit lower (16% / 24% / 15%).

🔍 Cold-Review Finding #2: Volume & Dock Skew

30 of the 37.5 pallets are farm loads. Weighted by pallets, assistance drops to 9% vs. 19% corpus mean because big loads can only go to the few recipients with room (14 have 5+ pallets; 10 have 10+ pallets).

8 · Honest Limitations & Roadmap (Judge Check #4) Slide 8 / 8

Explicit Boundaries Today & What We Build Next

A tool a coordinator can trust must state what it knows, what is generated, and where human verification is required.

⚠️ Four Honest Limitations Today

  • Generated Operational Fields & 9 Planted Cases: 183 org names/addresses/income are real IRS records; all storage, pallet capacities, and pickup windows are generated—so every brief advises confirming by phone before dispatch.
  • Consumer Shelf-Life Guidance: USDA FoodKeeper is home-storage guidance, not commercial cold-chain telemetry.
  • ZIP-Centroid Locations: Recipient coordinates are ZIP centroids joined to the nearest census tract internal point (ST_DISTANCE), and surplus_postings has no donor coordinates.
  • Small Sample & Stateless Memory: 10 postings across 68 tracts cannot separate skew from noise, and without delivery history one fast pantry wins repeated short-clock offers.

🚀 Production Next Steps

  • Persistent Delivery Rotation & Locks (Firestore — Issue #12): Record daily claims and declines so offers spread across viable pantries instead of calling Shakespear Rose three times.
  • Backend Multi-Recipient Load Splitting (Issue #13): Extend find_matches to automatically split 5-ton farm loads across 2–3 pantries when no single cooler has room.
  • Live Pantry Self-Onboarding (Issue #10): Use conversational intake to collect real walk-in cooler and pallet-jack capabilities directly into BigQuery + AI.EMBED.
