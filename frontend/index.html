          Common Questions
        


        

          
1. How does RescueBroker choose the best pantry?

          

            We check 80 nearby pantries that handle that type of food, then automatically filter out any location that doesn't have the right cooler, doesn't have enough open pallet space, or can't pick up before the food expires.
          

          
        


        

          
2. How much notice do Chicago pantries need for pickup?

          

            Across all 192 partner locations, the fastest pickup time is 2 hours (5 pantries). Given 6 hours, 78 pantries (41%) can receive the food. If a donation has less than 2 hours left, we flag it so a driver isn't sent on an impossible run.
          

        


        

          
3. Should I still call or text the pantry before sending a driver?

          

            Yes! Cooler space and hours on file can change during a busy day. Always send the pre-written text message or call to confirm before the truck rolls, and click Report Problem if their cooler is full or broken.
          

        


        

          
4. Why is unopened infant formula allowed when liquid dairy expires quickly?

          

            Standard food guidelines list 2–4 days for opened liquid formula, but factory-sealed cans are safe for months. When a donor confirms the cans are sealed and within their printed date, we honor the sealed expiration date.
          

        


        

          
5. How is neighborhood need used?

          

            Neighborhood poverty and car-access numbers are shown only to help you see who the rescue serves—they are never used to hold back or delay food from any pantry that can safely take it.
