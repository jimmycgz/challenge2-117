          Frequently Asked Questions
        


        

          
1. Why does vector search use `top_k = 80` before filtering?

          

            BigQuery `VECTOR_SEARCH` ranks by semantic meaning first. For the 6h spinach load, `top_k=15` leaves only 1 viable survivor after cold-storage & clock filters; `top_k=80` yields 12–14 viable pantries while catching semantic decoys like Greenline Community Pantry.
          

          
        


        

          
2. What is a realistic pickup window in Chicago?

          

            Across all 192 recipients, the fastest pickup lead time is 2.0 hours (only 5 pantries). At ≤6.0 hours, 78 pantries (40.6%) are reachable. Offers under 2 hours (like the 1h cream pastries case) trigger a safe refusal.
          

        


        

          
3. Are pantry cooler & pallet capacities real or synthetic?

          

            Organization names, addresses, and EINs are real IRS 990 nonprofits; `cold_storage`, `max_pallets`, and `pickup_window_hours` are synthetic ("profile on file"). Always confirm by phone before rolling a truck, and use Report Issue if a cooler is broken.
          

        


        

          
4. How does the Sealed Infant Formula rule work?

          

            USDA FoodKeeper lists `2–4 days` for opened liquid formula. Our `lookup_shelf_life` tool overrides this for unopened/sealed cans so 8-month shelf-stable infant formula isn't falsely rejected.
          

        


        

          
5. Are Census poverty stats used to rank recipients?

          

            Never. ACS tract poverty, no-vehicle, and public assistance rates are reported strictly as descriptive context in Part 4 of the brief and never alter candidate ranking.
